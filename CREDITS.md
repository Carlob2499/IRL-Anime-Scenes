## Anime frames

The “frame” side of each scene is **original SVG artwork interpreting the
shot** — actual film stills are copyrighted and are not distributed with this
repository. If you own screenshots you're comfortable using, drop them in as
`public/img/frames/<slug>.jpg` (`your-name`, `slam-dunk`, `garden-of-words`,
`spirited-away`, `steins-gate`, `silent-voice`) and the site will use them
automatically.
